Design Language · 2027

Built Around Autonomous

2027 design philosophy

FRC matches are shaped by autonomous, and our team is set up to deliver on it.

Mentors bring depth in strategy and autonomous software. Students bring strength in manufacturing and assembly, so we can build, repair, and deliver the robots autonomous testing depends on. Together we design a robot built for its auto paths, deliver the prototype and final robots as early as possible, and test autos on an accurate field in our own shop.

Built on

  • Mentor depth in strategy and autonomous software.
  • Student strength in manufacturing and assembly.
  • Auto outcomes carry into the rest of the match.
  • Auto testing fits the shop space we have.
ScopeThis is our philosophy for the 2027 season, set by our current constraints. We'll revisit it each season as people, space, and budget change.

Design Language · 2027

How We Deliver It

The robot, the schedule, and the field we test on.

The robot
Built for auto paths

Mechanism layout is chosen around the autonomous routine. Intake and shooter, or intake and end effector, face the directions that make auto paths shortest and cleanest.

The schedule
Robots early

Complex enough to enable software. Simple enough to deliver on time.

Deliver a software-testable prototype early, then the final robot as early as possible, for weeks of software work and auto tuning. Mechanical focuses on 1–1.5 mechanisms’ worth of robot, so the team ships a simple robot that can be tested for a long time.

The field
Accurate shop field

Build high-quality, accurate field elements sized to fit our shop, so autonomous is tested as close to the real field as possible.

Design Language · 2027

One Elite Mechanism

Robot complexity

We focus on 1–1.5 mechanisms’ worth of robot.

We deliver one elite mechanism, and that mechanism handles and delivers the autonomous performance we're after.

Why

  • We have traditionally not had success focusing on multiple mechanisms at once.
  • More can be added later. We leave room for upgrades throughout the season.
RuleComplex enough to enable software. Simple enough to deliver on time.

Alpha Bot and CAD Hell

Prove the geometry on a driving robot, then release CAD that fabrication can trust.

A robot that is accurate in geometry and materials, without most of the moving parts.

Releasing high-fidelity CAD lets fabrication and assembly stay focused, which plays to the students' strength in manufacturing and assembly.

2027 Season Plan · Strategy

Consistency Is the Game

How a TorBots robot plays

A TorBots robot wins on consistency.

We go for a consistent autonomous, and that usually means a highly automated teleop robot too. The robot is built to do the same thing well every match.

Driver practice

  • Practice is built around consistency, not peak performance.
  • It ramps up as auto and software get tuned and competition gets closer.
  • Without a full practice field, we don't plan on an explosive driver performance.

2027 Season Plan · Strategy

Alliance Selection

A consistent robot needs partners who can raise the ceiling.

Default to avoid
Consistent + consistentCeiling capped

We don't play in a high-variance way that can turn a match on our own. Pairing with another consistent team caps how high the alliance can go.

Exception: if a consistent partner is enough to win the event, take it.

Default pick
Consistent + high upsideChance to win

Pick higher-upside, riskier teams. Our consistency sets the floor, and their variance gives the alliance a real chance to win the event.

ScoutingScout and build pick lists with this in mind: look for each team's ceiling and upside, not only its average.
