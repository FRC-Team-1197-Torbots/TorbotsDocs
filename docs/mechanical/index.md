## Standards

How we design and build robots today. Each standard lists why we do it and which season taught us.

- [Power Transmission](standards/power-transmission.md): belts, pulleys, shafts, hubs, and ratios
- [Structure & Materials](standards/structure-materials.md): plate, pocketing, robot width, standoffs, friction, polycarb
- [Bearings & Retention](standards/bearings.md): retaining and aligning bearings
- [Linear Motion](standards/linear-motion.md): constraining anything that slides or extends
- [Intakes](standards/intakes.md): compliant vs. rigid, acquisition
- [Shooters](standards/shooters.md): prototyping before committing
- [3D Printing](standards/3d-printing.md): hubs, and what not to print
- [CAD](standards/cad.md): integration, parametric design, weight estimation
