# Bearings & Retention

How we seat and retain bearings today.

## Retention

- **Retain every bearing positively.** Use a bearing hat (retaining plate or flange), shear pins, or another mechanical retainer. Never rely on press fit or print tolerance alone.
  *Why:* print tolerance alone didn't hold bearings in, and we had to fall back on epoxy plus spacers on the dead axle setup. *([2026 Off-Season](../../seasons/2026/off-season.md))*
- **In printed bearing seats, design shear pins into the print.**
  *Why:* this would have kept the bearings in without the epoxy fix. *([2026 Off-Season](../../seasons/2026/off-season.md))*
- **Seat bearings in 1/4" plate where weight allows.**
  *Why:* 1/4" aluminum noticeably improved bearing retention over 1/8". See [Structure & Materials](structure-materials.md#plate). *([2026 Off-Season](../../seasons/2026/off-season.md))*

## Alignment

- **Don't let bearings be the only thing holding a mechanism in alignment.** The structure around them has to be rigid. See [Power Transmission: Belts](power-transmission.md#belts).
