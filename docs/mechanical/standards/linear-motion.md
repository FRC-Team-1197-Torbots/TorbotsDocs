# Linear Motion

How we guide anything that slides or extends today.

## Constrain Side to Side

- **Restrict side-to-side movement on every moving part.** The extra friction from guides is worth it.
  *Why:* loose belts, rack and pinion drives, and intake arms all failed in 2026 because nothing held them in line. *([2026 Off-Season](../../seasons/2026/off-season.md))*

## Rack and Pinion

- **Guide both sides of a rack and pinion tightly enough to stay on the rack**, especially when it's exposed outside the frame. Constraining one side isn't enough.
  *Why:* the exposed intake arm racks needed tight side-to-side control to stay engaged. *([2026 Off-Season](../../seasons/2026/off-season.md))*
- **Design slider blocks or guides in from the start,** not as a fix later.
  *Why:* we added them to the intake arms far later than we should have. *([2026 Off-Season](../../seasons/2026/off-season.md))*

## Guide Bearings

- **Don't go smaller than the 2026 off-season guide bearings,** and consider going bigger.
  *([2026 Off-Season](../../seasons/2026/off-season.md))*
