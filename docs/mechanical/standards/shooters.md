# Shooters

How we design shooters today.

## Prototype Before Committing

- **Prototype and measure compression ourselves before committing to a shooter design.** Treat other teams' numbers as a starting point only, and check that their setup matches ours.
  *Why:* we set hood compression from other teams' numbers without prototyping. Most of those teams didn't run a full-contact hood, so our compression ended up far higher than needed and hurt shooter performance. *([2026 Off-Season](../../seasons/2026/off-season.md))*
