# Structure & Materials

What we build robots out of today, and how we keep them stiff.

## Plate

- **Use 1/4" aluminum plate** over 1/8" when weight allows.
  *Why:* much better overall robot rigidity and bearing retention. *([2026 Off-Season](../../seasons/2026/off-season.md))*
- **McMaster oversized 1/4" aluminum plate is fine to buy.**
  *Why:* works just as well at a significant cost saving. It isn't as cheap as it used to be, but still saves money. *([2026 Off-Season](../../seasons/2026/off-season.md))*
- **Tap holes in 1/4" plate** instead of adding nuts or extra fasteners.
  *Why:* removes fasteners and simplifies integration. *([2026 Off-Season](../../seasons/2026/off-season.md))*

## Pocketing

- **Don't pocket high-stress areas or surfaces that take external contact.** Where you do pocket, keep the ribs substantial.
  *Why:* over-pocketing driven by weight worries caused internal and external damage, and we ended up underweight anyway. *([2026 Off-Season](../../seasons/2026/off-season.md))*
- Check the weight estimate before pocketing. See [CAD: Weight Estimation](cad.md#weight-estimation).

## Robot Width

- **A wide robot needs supports perpendicular to the long dimension.** Support long shafts and cross bars along their length.
  *Why:* a wide robot was great for intake and shooter throughput, but bad for defense and center of gravity. The long lever arm made unsupported shafts flex and long cross bars take a lot of damage. *([2026 Off-Season](../../seasons/2026/off-season.md))*

## Standoffs and Mounting

- **Use metal standoffs for motors**, not printed ones.
  *Why:* they come in standard sizes and don't creep. *([2026 Off-Season](../../seasons/2026/off-season.md))*

## Friction Reduction

- **Use UHMW tape** wherever we need to reduce sliding friction.
  *([2026 Off-Season](../../seasons/2026/off-season.md))*

## Polycarbonate

- **For a part that must stay curved, use very thin polycarb (1/32").** Don't rely on thicker polycarb's stiffness to hold a curve.
  *Why:* thicker curved pieces didn't keep the shape we needed. *([2026 Off-Season](../../seasons/2026/off-season.md))*
- For polycarb as a spring in intakes, see [Intakes](intakes.md).
