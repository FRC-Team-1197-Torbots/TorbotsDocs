# 3D Printing

How we design and print robot parts today.

## Hubs

- **Use a tapered ceiling** in printed hubs.
  *Why:* makes the hub easier to print. *([2026 Off-Season](../../seasons/2026/off-season.md))*
- **Put nut access holes on the outside of the tube** where possible.
  *([2026 Off-Season](../../seasons/2026/off-season.md))*
- **Print high-torque or high-RPM hubs in Onyx or another high-grade engineering material.**
  *Why:* so the hub survives. *([2026 Off-Season](../../seasons/2026/off-season.md))*

## Bearings in Prints

- Print tolerance alone won't hold a bearing. See [Bearings & Retention](bearings.md#retention).

## Pulleys

- Printed pulleys with print inserts are fine if they stay aligned. See [Power Transmission: Pulleys](power-transmission.md#pulleys).

## What Not to Print

- **Motor standoffs.** Use metal; printed standoffs creep. See [Structure & Materials](structure-materials.md#standoffs-and-mounting).
